Thoughts of a Student

Essays written from inside the classroom, with the library open and no hurry. Here we write the essays that ethics and our own way of looking allow us to write: about method, about what is taught and what isn't, about what it takes to hold someone else's process. They are not lecture notes or textbook truths; they are the questions of someone still learning to ask them well.
